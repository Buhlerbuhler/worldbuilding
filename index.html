THERE IS NO SINGLE RIGHT FORMAT.

Write, draw, design, map, explain, build, or combine approaches. Your job is to add meaning to the world.

HOW IT WORKS

Four guiding moves
01 / INVENT

Create

Add something original: a character, coffee shop, planet, team, law, artifact, scene, map, or your own idea.

02 / CONNECT

Belong

Show how your creation connects to the original story or to another contribution in the archive.

03 / DEVELOP

Go deeper

Think through the details. What does your creation reveal about the world? Why does it matter?

04 / EXPLAIN

Show your thinking

Be ready to explain what you made, the choices you made, and what you would explore next.

BEFORE YOU FINISH

Submission / reflection

Turn in

Your planning sheet, your finished worldbuilding contribution, and your individual reflection.

Reflect

Why did you choose this? How does it connect? What are you proud of? What would you improve or explore further?

OUR AGREEMENT

Ideas can be unfinished. Ask questions before criticizing. Don't make fun of someone else's work. Ask permission before changing or using another person's creation. Everyone gets to decide how to share.
