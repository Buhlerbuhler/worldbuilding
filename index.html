THE PROJECT

WORLD BUILDING / GRADE 8 ELA

Create something that expands Jax’s World. Write, draw, design, explain, research, or combine approaches.

01 / ORIGINAL

ADD SOMETHING

Add something the story and class have not already fully explained. Your contribution should make the world larger.

02 / CONNECTED

MAKE IT BELONG

Connect your contribution to the original story or something else that already exists in our shared world.

03 / DEVELOPED

PUSH IT FURTHER

Include enough details that someone else can understand how your idea works, why it exists, and why it matters.

04 / PURPOSEFUL

MAKE CHOICES

Use your chosen format intentionally. Be able to explain why you made important choices and what they add to the world.
